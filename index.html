Key Conceptual Distinction (HSC Exam Tip):
The Heading Angle (θ) is measured from the straight-across direction (y-axis) relative to the water. The Path Angle (α) is the boat's actual trajectory angle measured from the river bank in the ground frame. Students frequently confuse these two — always check which frame of reference a question uses.
